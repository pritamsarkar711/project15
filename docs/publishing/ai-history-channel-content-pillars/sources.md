Every one is an official platform page, a recognised creator education resource, or a dedicated industry analysis.

## Research notes

- The content pillar framework in the article is based on recurring patterns in long running history channels such as Kings and Generals, History Hit, The Armchair Historian, and Fall of Civilizations, combined with current creator guidance on evergreen history content.
- The article treats AI as a production aid, not as a replacement for verification and a clear editorial voice. That matches the platform direction visible in the enforcement cases referenced in the source list.
- No fabricated statistics or invented case studies are used. Numbers and revenue claims were deliberately avoided because they change quickly and vary by sub niche and audience geography.
- Specific data points such as enforcement timelines should be rechecked before a later update, because platform policies keep changing.
