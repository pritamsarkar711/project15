Huvanti — a complete Laravel blog CMS with SEO built in

Live SEO scoring while you write, IndexNow instant indexing, automatic social media publishing and an AI writing assistant — one self-hosted Laravel application.

See it live at huvanti.com View source on GitHub

Everything a modern blog needs

Huvanti powers huvanti.com in production — every article there is written, reviewed, SEO-scored, indexed and social-shared through this codebase.

Live SEO scoring

A RankMath-style 0–100 score runs in the editor as you type: focus keyword placement, density, title and meta lengths, subheadings, links and image alt text.

Instant indexing

IndexNow pings Bing, Yandex, Seznam and Naver the moment a post is published, updated or deleted — plus one-click manual indexing buttons.

Social automation

Publish once and Huvanti shares to X, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn, Instagram, Telegram and Pinterest automatically, with a delivery log and retries.

AI writing assistant

Bring your own key (NVIDIA NIM free tier, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI). One-click titles, descriptions, keywords, excerpts and FAQs with automatic model failover.

Author workflow

Draft → submit → admin review → publish, with returned-post notes, resubmission and a dedicated author dashboard.

Full CMS

Categories with drag & drop, pages, navigation manager, comment moderation, ads manager, dark mode and site-wide font controls.

Tech stack

Laravel 13 · PHP 8.3+ · Tailwind CSS 4 · Vite 8 · MySQL or SQLite · zero-dependency custom editor.
